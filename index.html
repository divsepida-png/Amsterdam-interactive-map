Best routes to the hotel

From Centraal, Dam or Leidseplein: Tram 17 Take Tram 17 toward Surinameplein, leave at Surinameplein, then walk about 500 m to Hendrikje Stoffelsstraat 1.
From Weesperplein, Weteringcircuit or Leidseplein: Tram 1 Take Tram 1 toward Surinameplein. The final walk is approximately 500 m.
From Station Zuid or Sloterdijk: Metro 51 Take Metro 51 to Amsterdam Lelylaan, then walk approximately 8–10 minutes. During the current summer timetable, Metro 51 does not serve Centraal, Nieuwmarkt or Waterlooplein.
